COURSE DATA

The courses are made from OpenStreetMap data.
  (C) OpenStreetMap contributors. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  The course files in assets/holes, assets/courses and assets/course_worlds
  are derived from it and are available under the Open Database License
  (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The ground heights of the courses in Denmark come from the Danish Elevation
Model.
  Data: Danmarks Hoejdemodel (DHM/Terraen), dataforsyningen.dk.
  Data owner: Klimadatastyrelsen.
